# Rainforest textures

- [Bark Brown 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02) · Poly Haven · CC0. Diffuse map resized to 768 px and recompressed as `bark.jpg`.
- [Forest Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor) · Poly Haven · CC0. Diffuse map resized to 768 px and recompressed as `forest-floor.jpg`.

- [Forest Leaves 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_leaves_02) · Rob Tuytel · CC0. Diffuse map resized to 768 px and recompressed as `moss-litter.jpg`.

Leaf veins, moss tufts, and canyon geometry are generated in the game.

## Sound

- [Forest edge calm atmosphere at dusk](https://freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/671340/) · Félix Blume · CC0. A section of the Amazon rainforest recording was filtered, crossfaded for looping, and encoded as `dusk-ambience.mp3`.
